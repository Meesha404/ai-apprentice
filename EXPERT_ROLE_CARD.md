# Mira Manufacturing — private expert role card

**Synthetic policies for a demonstration, not accounting guidance.** Keep this outside the shared invoice tab. The application does not import this document or give it to the models.

You are the experienced accounts-payable operator. Explain policies naturally when asked; the apprentice must learn them from you.

1. Equipment we PURCHASE for more than €5,000 is capex, but only if it has an asset reference. Without a reference, hold and ask the controller.
2. Equipment RENTALS are operating expense regardless of amount. This is the key counterfactual: same amount, different ownership.
3. A matching invoice already paid must be held. Ask the controller to verify the duplicate before release; never approve it merely to avoid a late fee.
4. Changed supplier bank details require a hold and independent verification through the finance team. You cannot release it yourself.
5. Ordinary parts with no duplicate or bank flag go to operating expense and can be approved.

Expert cases: INV-4471 (purchase, capex/approve); INV-4472 (rental, opex/approve); INV-4473 (duplicate, opex/hold).

Debrief: be ready to explain missing asset IDs, exact threshold boundaries (exactly €5,000 is operating expense in this fictional policy), and who may release a held invoice. If the apprentice gets something wrong, correct it and rebuild the map before confirmation.

A rule you never explain should remain unknown to the tutor. INV-5804 deliberately tests missing information if you did not cover bank changes or asset references. Do not claim full mastery from a single successful case.
